PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

ZEN-BU Inc. ("we" or "the Company") handles the personal information entrusted to us through inquiries, applications, and other interactions in the course of operating SHINDO ("this site") in accordance with the following policy.

1. Definition of personal information

"Personal information" means information about a living individual that can identify a specific individual by the name, date of birth, or other descriptions it contains, or that can easily be cross-referenced with other information and thereby identify a specific individual.

2. Collection of personal information

We may collect personal information when you contact us through this site, consult us about visits or relocation, apply for a position, or similar. We collect it by lawful and fair means after stating the purpose of use. The personal information we collect is as follows.

  • Name and its reading
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Postal address
  • Records and contents of your correspondence with us
  • Information that can identify a specific individual when combined with the above

3. Purposes of use

We use the personal information entrusted to us for the following purposes.

  • Responding to inquiries and consultations
  • Communications about recruitment and hosting
  • Providing information about our activities

We do not disclose or provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except in the following cases.

  • Where required by law, or where cooperation is needed for a national or local government body, or a party entrusted by one, to carry out duties prescribed by law
  • Where necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property and it is difficult to obtain your consent

4. Use of external services

Information entered in the application form on this site is processed on servers we manage and sent to our staff through the email delivery service provided by Resend, Inc. This site is hosted using services provided by Vercel Inc.

Some inquiries and applications are received through Google Forms, provided by Google LLC. Information entered in those forms is stored on Google's servers. For how Google handles it, please see Google's privacy policy.

This site also uses Google Fonts, provided by the same company, for its display.

5. Security of personal information

We strive to prevent unauthorized access to, and loss, alteration, or leakage of, the personal information entrusted to us, and handle it appropriately in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Disclosure, correction, and deletion

For inquiries about the protection of personal information, or requests for disclosure, correction, or deletion, please use the contact details below. After confirming your identity, we will respond promptly.

7. Contact

ZEN-BU Inc.
Email: official@zen-bu.co

8. Changes to this policy

When we change the personal information we collect, its purposes of use, or any other part of this policy, we announce the change by updating this page.
