PROJECTS

What we are doing,
what we have done, what comes next

A list of the actual projects on the ground.
Sorted into three groups: ongoing, complete, and upcoming.

Ongoing

2026– ｜ with Munokai Natural Farm ｜ Showa Village

Launching a new production base

Starting a new natural farm from scratch in a village at the headwaters. We are developing it as a base for rice, soybeans, and vegetables.

2026– ｜ with local contractors ｜ Showa Village

Reviving abandoned farmland

Clearing paddies left idle for several years and bringing rice back. This village sits at the very top of the water cycle, at the source.

2026– ｜ with Daiku Hachimaru ｜ Showa Village

Building a new shared lodge

Restoring an old house to welcome families with children. We are developing it as a base where everyone can stay overnight.

2025– ｜ with Iwashiro-kuni Kazemusubi ｜ Isasumi Shrine, Aizumisato

Revitalizing the natural environment

Caring for springs, streams, mountains, and soil. Year-round work with Iwashiro-kuni Kazemusubi, including the restoration of the sacred grove at Isasumi Shrine.

2026– ｜ with Munokai Natural Farm ｜ Showa Village

No-till, low-input natural rice farming

Trialing rice cultivation that does not depend on large tractors, in a heavy-snow region at 800 m elevation. The near-term target is 360 kg per 1,000 m².

with Trade-On Co., Ltd. ｜ Aizumisato

Developing and maintaining farm machinery

From sourcing used machinery and equipment to repair and installation. This underpins our premise of small, low-cost machines suited to hilly terrain.

with YouBeYou Inc. ｜ Aizumisato

Circulating Future Being／CFB

A working community that connects city and farm around a circular way of life. Together we design relationships that let everyone involved keep standing on the production site.
Ongoing ｜ Aizu and the Tokyo area

Programs for students

Elementary school classes, high school camps, and joint research with universities. We open the village as a place for learning.

with G-with Inc. ｜ Motobu, Okinawa

Succession support for an organic mango farm

Under the banner of IKIGAI — a society where people can live happily to the end — newcomers are taking over the farm. It made a fresh start as a new farm in 2026.
Year-round ｜ with Munokai Natural Farm ｜ mainly in busy seasons

Farm volunteering and visits

From a few days at a time, during busy periods such as rice planting and harvest. There is no limit on numbers.
Event ｜ with Munokai Natural Farm ｜ once a year, in autumn

Harvest festival

A day to share the year's harvest with everyone who took part. Partners are welcome to stay overnight.

Complete

2023–2024 ｜ with funding partners across Japan

Raised ¥160 million in the first year

We presented the business vision directly to more than 400 people, and within a year 37 private partners had joined.

2024 ｜ Aizumisato ｜ with Shiki Co., Ltd.

Restoring our base, Rikū

A 250-year-old house on the verge of demolition, restored over two years with Aizu craftsmen. This is where SHINDO began.

2025 ｜ with Munokai Natural Farm ｜ Aizumisato

Succeeded in no-till, no-weeding soybean cultivation

Sowed about 700 m² in two hours with a hand-pushed seeder. Without weeding once, the yield exceeded the prefectural conventional average.

2026 ｜ with Trade-On Co., Ltd. ｜ Showa Village

Restoring a farm warehouse

A large steel-frame warehouse in Onogawa, its walls crumbling and its interior piled with junk. From cleaning to wall work and concrete repairs, we brought it back into use.

Spring 2026, Showa Village

Farmland restored by 65 people

Sixty-five people from one company gathered from across Japan to restore abandoned farmland in Showa Village.

2025 ｜ with Trade-On Co., Ltd. ｜ Aizumisato

Building a roller crimper for no-till soybeans

A key tool for no-till farming that flattens cover crops into mulch instead of mowing them. We built it ourselves rather than importing one.

2025– ｜ with Trade-On Co., Ltd. ｜ in testing

Converting arice transplanterfor no-till use

A small, low-cost seeder that small and mid-sized farms can afford, and a rice transplanter converted for no-till. Developed jointly with Trade-On.

with YouBeYou Inc. ｜ 2025

Half-farming, half-business program

A system in which city residents come to Munokai throughout the year to support the farm. Now in its second year, it has welcomed new members.

FY2025 ｜ with Shiki Co., Ltd. ｜ Aizumisato

Completed a rice storage warehouse

By converting a storehouse into a mid-sized warehouse, we can now keep 2.4 tons of brown rice refrigerated even through summer.

2025 ｜ Aizumisato

Hands-on help planting no-till soybeans

Company staff headed into the fields with hand-pushed seeders, supporting the planting of no-till, no-weeding soybeans by hand.

with Ayako Mogi & Naoya Sasaki ｜ Aizumisato

Documenting on film

Produced the concept film SHINDO 2025. Director Ayako Mogi moved to Aizu in 2026.

July–August 2026 ｜ Aizumisato

Building an earthen stove and earth oven

Over 17 days, more than 100 people in total kneaded clay to build a two-burner earthen stove and an earth oven in the earthen-floored gatehouse at Rikū.

with Healing Connections ｜ Aizumisato

Coherence Circle in Aizu

A four-day program in which people from diverse backgrounds sit in a circle and create harmony through dialogue and bodily awareness.
