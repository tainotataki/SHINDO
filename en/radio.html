RADIO ── Yone Yone Radio

Yone Yone Radio for SHINDO

Yone Yone Radio is a podcast of conversations among the people involved in SHINDO, who are building a path toward a new kind of abundance. Based in Aizu, Fukushima, they talk plainly and at length about farming, everyday life, and the foundations of the society to come.

The voices of people standing in the field, delivered at their own temperature. Listen on your commute or while you work.
